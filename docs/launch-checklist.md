# Premium Timber Hub - MVP Launch Checklist

## Release rule

Do not publish because the pages merely look complete. Launch only when the required business facts, enquiry flow, technical checks and legal basics below are complete.

The MVP specification is the source of truth where this checklist and another legacy document conflict.

## 1. Business facts and product claims

- [ ] Legal seller and privacy-controller identity are confirmed.
- [ ] Legal company name, registration number and public business address are confirmed.
- [ ] The official enquiry recipient mailbox and responsible human owner are confirmed.
- [ ] Natural Abachi formats, profiles, dimensions, lengths, finish and grades are confirmed, or the page uses only approved enquiry-based wording.
- [ ] Thermo Abachi available lengths and dimensional tolerances are confirmed, or omitted.
- [ ] All published Raw Abachi dimensions, grades and permitted defects are confirmed against the current specification.
- [ ] Actual stock location and stock policy are confirmed before publishing `EU stock` or similar claims.
- [ ] Feasible order quantities, sample policy and delivery model are confirmed before describing them.
- [ ] Pricing CTA matches reality: no `Price List` promise unless a current B2B price list can actually be supplied.
- [ ] A realistic enquiry-response commitment is approved before it is displayed.

## 2. Documentation, quality and compliance

- [ ] Specific certificates, origin/legality and traceability documents have been checked for validity before naming them publicly.
- [ ] No FSC logo or FSC claim is present.
- [ ] `European standards`, `responsible sourcing` and `controlled supply chain` are absent unless evidence and approved wording exist.
- [ ] Thermal-treatment wording matches actual process documentation; no waterproof or maintenance-free implication appears.
- [ ] Responsibility for drying, thermal treatment, profiling and quality control is accurately described.
- [ ] Product tolerances, moisture data and grade definitions are only shown where confirmed.

## 3. Content and imagery

- [ ] Hero clearly identifies Abachi, sauna application, European B2B audience and the commercial next step.
- [ ] Natural, Thermo and Raw Abachi have distinct product paths; Raw Abachi does not dominate the website.
- [ ] All unverified information is omitted or uses approved safe wording; no visible `[placeholder]` text remains.
- [ ] Every image is approved for use and correctly contextualised.
- [ ] Stock or AI images are not presented as Premium Timber Hub facilities, shipments or completed projects.
- [ ] Client names, logos, testimonials and project images have explicit publication permission.
- [ ] Product images use meaningful alt text; decorative images use empty alt text.
- [ ] English copy has been reviewed for accuracy and consistent product terminology: Abachi, Obeche and Ayous.

## 4. Forms, sales flow and assistant readiness

- [ ] Every commercial CTA opens or leads to the working enquiry flow.
- [ ] The form captures name, company, business email, country, customer type, product, dimensions/profile, quantity, required date and delivery location.
- [ ] CTA intent, product source page and UTM data are retained with the lead.
- [ ] Required consent, success state and error state work.
- [ ] Spam protection works without blocking legitimate submissions.
- [ ] File uploads are restricted to safe file types and sizes.
- [ ] A real public-site form submission is delivered to the correct mailbox and checked for spam-folder placement.
- [ ] The sales assistant has an approved mailbox access path and a defined human approval owner.
- [ ] The assistant does not send emails, quotes or commercial commitments without human approval.

## 5. Technical quality and accessibility

- [ ] Pages are tested on current phone, tablet and desktop layouts.
- [ ] No horizontal scrolling, hidden CTA, broken menu or overlapping content exists.
- [ ] The hero image is appropriately compressed and does not create an avoidable LCP delay.
- [ ] Below-the-fold media uses responsive sizes and lazy loading where appropriate.
- [ ] No autoplay video with sound is present.
- [ ] Each page has one H1, semantic heading order, readable contrast, visible keyboard focus and real form labels.
- [ ] Keyboard navigation and form validation have been tested.
- [ ] No production credentials, API keys or internal data appear in theme or page code.

## 6. SEO, privacy and production configuration

- [ ] Canonical URLs use `https://premiumtimberhub.eu`.
- [ ] Every page has a unique title, meta description and social-share image.
- [ ] Canonical tags, `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt` and 404 behaviour have been checked.
- [ ] Redirects from replaced legacy URLs work; `.co.uk` consistently redirects to `.eu`.
- [ ] Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy and Terms of Use reflect the actual legal seller, form handling and analytics.
- [ ] Cookie consent blocks non-essential analytics/marketing scripts until consent where required.
- [ ] SMTP or transactional email is configured with valid SPF, DKIM and DMARC for the sending domain.
- [ ] Production form delivery is tested after DNS and mail configuration are live.
- [ ] Analytics events are tested where enabled: quote start/submit, sample request start/submit, availability submit, email click and phone click.

## 7. Final release approval

- [ ] Business owner approves final product information, wording and imagery.
- [ ] Responsible person approves the enquiry mailbox, sales workflow and response ownership.
- [ ] All blockers above are resolved, or the corresponding claim/function has been removed from the public site.
- [ ] A post-launch review date is set for 30-60 days after launch to evaluate enquiry quality and decide the next expansion.
