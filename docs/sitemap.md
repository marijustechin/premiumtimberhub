# Premium Timber Hub - MVP Sitemap

## Purpose

Premium Timber Hub is an English-language B2B website for Abachi products used by sauna manufacturers, distributors and interior professionals in Europe. The MVP is a small product-and-enquiry website, not a single-page export landing page or e-commerce shop.

The current MVP specification overrides any earlier anchor-only sitemap.

## Public pages

| Page | URL | Purpose | Primary CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home | `/` | Explain the B2B Abachi offer, prioritise finished Natural and Thermo products, and route visitors into a qualified enquiry. | Request a Quote |
| Products | `/products` | Give a concise overview of Natural Abachi, Thermo Abachi and Kiln-Dried Raw Abachi. | View product / Request a Quote |
| Natural Abachi | `/products/natural-abachi` | Describe confirmed Natural Abachi formats and sauna/interior applications. Unknown formats remain enquiry-dependent. | Check Availability |
| Thermo Abachi | `/products/thermo-abachi` | Present Thermo Abachi as a distinct premium product line, with confirmed STS 3D / 20 mm / 80 mm coverage data. | Request a Quote |
| Kiln-Dried Raw Abachi | `/products/raw-abachi` | Offer kiln-dried rough-sawn Abachi to importers, manufacturers and profiling companies. | Check Availability |
| Contact | `/contact` | Provide one dependable B2B enquiry form and business contact details once confirmed. | Submit enquiry |

## Homepage structure

1. **Hero** - Abachi, sauna application, Europe and B2B audience immediately clear.
2. **Product paths** - Natural Abachi, Thermo Abachi and Kiln-Dried Raw Abachi.
3. **Why Premium Timber Hub** - evidence-led benefits only.
4. **From raw material to finished application** - a short, truthful process sequence.
5. **Applications** - sauna benches, wall and ceiling cladding, backrests, interior slat walls and custom profiles only where product availability supports the statement.
6. **B2B supply and enquiry block** - pricing/availability are enquiry-based.
7. **Final CTA** - Request a Quote / Request Samples.

## Enquiry actions

The MVP uses three commercial actions only:

1. `Request a Quote`
2. `Request Samples`
3. `Check Availability`

They may use the same form, provided the selected intent and product page are retained in the lead data.

## Deferred pages and categories

Do not create these pages in the MVP unless their content is genuinely ready:

- For Professionals
- Supply & Quality
- About Abachi
- Insights / blog
- Distributor programme
- Outdoor hardwood
- Stock portal
- E-commerce and public pricing
- Other timber species

Later additions must have distinct, substantive content and must not duplicate the current product pages.

## URL and SEO rules

- Use the canonical public domain: `https://premiumtimberhub.eu`.
- Keep URLs short, lower-case and stable.
- Give each public page one unique H1, title, meta description and social-share image.
- Maintain canonical URLs, `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt` and redirects from removed legacy URLs.
- The `.co.uk` domain must continue to redirect consistently to the canonical `.eu` URL structure.
