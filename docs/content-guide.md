# Premium Timber Hub - Content Guide

## Audience and tone

Write concise, professional business English for sauna manufacturers, installers, distributors and interior professionals in Europe.

- Lead with product, application and B2B relevance.
- Be specific where facts are confirmed; be neutral where commercial detail remains open.
- Prefer short paragraphs, plain language and action-led CTAs.
- Do not use consumer-shop language, hype, exotic sourcing imagery or generic superlatives.

## Product terminology

- Main name: `Abachi`.
- First explanatory mention where relevant: `Abachi (also known as Obeche or Ayous)`.
- Use `Natural Abachi`, `Thermo Abachi` and `Kiln-Dried Raw Abachi` consistently.
- Use `cladding`, `bench boards`, `backrest slats` and `custom profiles` only where the product availability supports the term.
- Do not describe Thermo Abachi as lower-grade material made darker.

## Approved content principles

- Prices, availability, dimensions, lead time and delivery are confirmed with the enquiry where they are not yet documented.
- Product claims must be supported by the MVP specification or company-information file.
- One product page has one clear H1, a concise summary, confirmed technical details, applications and a B2B CTA.
- Use real images as evidence; label contextual application imagery accurately.

## Prohibited until documented

- `European standards`, `EU stock`, `fast delivery`, `responsible sourcing` or `controlled supply chain`.
- FSC or broad certification language.
- Public guarantees about thermal performance, durability, moisture resistance, stock, MOQ or lead time.
- Invented clients, projects, statistics, volumes, years of operation or testimonials.
- Visible square-bracket placeholders in public content.

## Safe wording examples

- `Available formats and dimensions are confirmed with your enquiry.`
- `Contact us for current availability and lead time.`
- `Order quantities are agreed individually.`
- `B2B pricing depends on product, dimensions, quantity and delivery location.`
- `Request samples to assess the material and finish.`
